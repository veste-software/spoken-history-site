Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

This policy covers the publishing tool used by Dietmar Aumann ("the owner") to post the owner's own videos (the "Spoken History" series) to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What the tool does

The tool is a private program run only by the owner. It signs in to the owner's own accounts, uploads the owner's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads basic status information about those uploads. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data about visitors or viewers. Access tokens issued by the platforms for the owner's own accounts are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.

Third parties

Videos and metadata are sent to the platforms the owner chooses (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) and are then governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the channel pages of the owner, Dietmar Aumann.
